## Three independent decisions

- **Real broad allocation.** Needs a supported ITOT/IVV/alternative versus cash preference, a suitable
  allocation, and verified account and funds for any size. It needs no tactical Grade A, no 15% forecast
  and no short bracket.
- **Validated real tactical recommendation.** Needs a validated Grade A, a fresh complete live plan and
  appropriate account evidence. The objective is 15% net per trade within six calendar months, never a
  guarantee. Recommendations only.
- **AI-agent paper experiment.** Only the existing authorized AI-agent PAPER account. Grades A/B/C are
  permitted with an explicit contemporary hypothesis, a defensible complete server plan, costs, a time
  exit and risk checks. Actually submit when qualified. Label unvalidated experiments, and never advise
  copying them into real accounts.

## Prohibitions

Never:
- place real orders;
- buy options or futures;
- enroll in margin;
- operate baseline or autonomous accounts;
- create a new paper account or service;
- loosen existing stops;
- force trades.

The standing paper authorization is specific to this user and this workflow. Do not generalize it to
other users or accounts, and do not require daily reconfirmation of authorized paper orders.

## Paper sizing (user policy, not tunable after seeing outcomes)

Quantity is the smallest whole-share limit across:
- $1,000 initial notional and 1% of current paper equity per position;
- 0.25% of equity for the planned entry-to-stop loss plus modeled costs;
- 10% aggregate experiment gross exposure, including open and reserved orders;
- verified free cash and collateral, and every stricter server limit.

Further rules:
- At most two new positions per completed-session decision, across reruns.
- Reject the order if even one share doesn't fit.
- Short proceeds are not free cash.
- Planned stop risk is not maximum loss.
- Grade B/C does not itself fail an experiment. Grade insufficient, no current hypothesis, a failed
  adopted policy or an incomplete plan does.
- Idempotency comes from account + symbol + direction + plan version + target session, never from
  wall-clock retry time.

## Delivery

At most 120 words and three items:
1. the next real purchase or preference, or the named candidate and the exact missing gate;
2. the actual paper actions and results, and both directional verdicts;
3. concrete progress or blocker, plus the full-details link.

Label experiments "PAPER EXPERIMENT — unvalidated; not a real-money recommendation."

## Skill maintenance

The user authorizes maintaining this workflow as experience accumulates, without another approval for
ordinary authorized maintenance. Never silently:
- expand authority;
- relax caps;
- turn research into orders;
- change schedules;
- replace frozen forecasts;
- adopt a failed strategy;
- tune numeric policy after seeing outcomes.

If saving an improvement fails, report it and keep the proposed diff in that run's evidence. Never claim
installation succeeded.
