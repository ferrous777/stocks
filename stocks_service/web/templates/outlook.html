Umpires annotate, never hide: the vote compares today's setup with the 50 most similar past setups (a veto means at least 61.8% of them were among the worst 23.6% of outcomes); each side shows its walk-forward verdict, and a vote from an umpire marked "not useful" should not be relied on. Labels are sampled every 5 sessions from development history before the sealed holdout and overlap in time, so n overstates the independent evidence.
