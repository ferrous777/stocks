How an agent should use it

{% call ui.card() %}
  1. Identify itself on every call. Every tool requires three arguments, logged exactly as sent: agent_id (a fixed name for the app, e.g. chatgpt), model_id (the exact model identifier only if the app's runtime provides it, otherwise unknown; never guessed or derived from the app's name) and prompt_version (v1). Calls without them are rejected.
  2. Read the outlook with get_outlook: one row per symbol and side, with the plan (entry limit, stop, target), expected six-month return, percentiles and evidence grade, and placeable_until.
  3. Check the account with get_account (cash, free buying power, positions).
  4. Place orders before the next open with place_paper_order, giving a unique idempotency_key (at least 8 characters; reusing it returns the same order) and a rationale. Orders are DAY limit orders at the row's entry limit, with its stop and target; they are refused after placeable_until, above the account's risk limits, or when the symbol already has an open order or position.
  5. Upload holdings directly; ask before watch-list changes. upload_holdings applies immediately and reports any differences from your records. add_to_watchlist and remove_from_watchlist first return a preview and a single-use confirm_token (valid 15 minutes, bound to those exact arguments) and change nothing. The agent shows you the preview; only after you agree does it call again with the token.
  6. Link to what it shows. Results carry links to the matching page; get_links builds others, such as a chart with calendar years overlaid (compare_years) or one range (chart_range, from_year).
  7. Review results with list_accounts, get_performance (return, drawdown, win rate, equity curve and the paired comparison with the baseline, including the influence gate), list_orders and list_transactions.
{% endcall %}
