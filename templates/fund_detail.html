Understanding Risk Metrics

Beta: Volatility relative to the market. Beta < 1 means less volatile than S&P 500.

Alpha: Excess return vs benchmark. Positive alpha indicates outperformance.

R-Squared: How closely the fund tracks the benchmark. Higher = more correlated.

Std Deviation: Annualized volatility. Lower means more stable returns.

Downside Dev: Volatility of negative returns only.

Sortino Ratio: Risk-adjusted return using only downside risk. Higher is better.
