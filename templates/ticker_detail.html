Understanding the Metrics
Win Rate: Percentage of trades that made money. >50% means more winners than losers.
Sharpe Ratio: Risk-adjusted return. >1 = good, >2 = very good. Higher means better returns per unit of risk.
Trend Strategy: Buys when price is rising, sells when falling. Works best in trending markets.
Momentum Strategy: Buys stocks accelerating upward. Higher risk/reward, shorter timeframe.
Mean Reversion: Buys oversold stocks expecting bounce back. Works when prices deviate from average.
BUY/SELL/HOLD: Current signal from each strategy based on latest data.
